# Mind Weaver — downloads

Dump the mess in. Your AI pet turns it into a workspace you can read as a
mind map, board, table, doc, gantt, calendar, gallery, chart or slides.

**[⬇ Download the latest build](../../releases/latest)** · [mindweaver.space](https://mindweaver.space)

---

## Windows

Portable — no installer. Download the `.exe`, put it anywhere, run it.

**Windows will warn you the app is unsigned.** Click **More info** → **Run anyway**.
Code signing is on the way; until then this is expected, and I'd rather say so
than have you wonder.

## Mac

Not yet. It's next.

---

## What you get without an account

- **Unlimited local weaves** — plain files on your disk, no login at all
- **Bring your own key** — paste a Gemini key and the AI runs on your key, not my meter
- **MCP server** — point Claude Desktop, Claude Code or Cursor at your folder and
  they can read and edit your weaves directly

Sign in only if you want cloud sync across devices.

---

## Your files

A weave is a `.weave` file in a folder you choose. Copy it, back it up, put it in
Dropbox, open it from another machine. Deleting the app does not delete your work.
